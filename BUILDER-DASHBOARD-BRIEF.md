# Builder Dashboard Brief

## Core business idea
The service acts like a construction materials concierge for builders and subcontractors.

Instead of calling many suppliers, checking prices, and chasing deliveries themselves, builders can send a request and let the business handle it.

The business:
- compares pricing across suppliers
- finds the best place to buy
- coordinates shipping and delivery timing
- gives updates on where each order stands
- accepts requests through WhatsApp
- can review blueprints and help identify needed materials

The goal is simple: the builder should spend less time sourcing materials and more time working on the job site.

## First page to build now
Focus only on the page a builder sees after logging in.

This page should answer:
- what project am I working on?
- what material orders do I have?
- what stage is each order in right now?
- what can I do next from here?

## Minimal first version
Use a minimal design.

The logged-in dashboard should include:
- project overview
- material order summary
- step/status tracking
- quick actions: request pricing, place material order, upload blueprint, message on WhatsApp, track shipping
- clear next step for the builder

## Tone
- practical
- clean
- contractor-friendly
- no fluff
- no fake future promises on screen
